Crocodiles

Crocodiles are large reptiles that live in rivers, lakes, and wetlands across Africa, Asia, and the Americas.

Crocodiles can grow up to 20 feet long and are known for their powerful jaws, which can generate more bite force than any other animal on Earth.

There are 13 known species of crocodile, with the saltwater crocodile being the largest, capable of living in both freshwater rivers and coastal ocean waters.

Crocodiles have a transparent third eyelid that lets them see underwater while keeping their eyes protected.
